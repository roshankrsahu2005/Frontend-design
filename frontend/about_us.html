Executive Architectural Blueprint & Operational Scope

The North Eastern Region (NER) of India represents one of the world's most complex logistical operating environments, characterized by high seismic vulnerability, steep topographical gradients, monsoonal precipitation extremes, and recurring landslide hazards along key arteries like the Siliguri Corridor and mountain passes of Assam, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Manipur, Nagaland, Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura, and Sikkim. Responding to SIH 26002, NE-AURA integrates real-time geospatial intelligence, predictive AI routing (ST-GCN + GRU), multi-modal modal switching, edge-native offline synchronization (Delta-CRDT), localized Bhashini voice accessibility, and national ULIP digital public infrastructure.

6 Core Architectural Engine Components

1. ST-GCN + GRU Risk Engine

Combines Spatial-Temporal Graph Convolutional Networks with Gated Recurrent Units to dynamically evaluate edge hazard scores ($R_{edge}$) using 72-hour ISRO SAR radar displacement, slope gradient, and rainfall indices.

2. Multi-Modal Swap Engine

Automatically reallocates freight across overland road networks, broad-gauge rail sidings (FOIS), National Waterway river barges (NW-2 Brahmaputra & NW-16 Barak), and high-altitude drone staging points.

3. National ULIP Gateway

Direct REST and webhook integration middleware connecting with VAHAN 4.0 (vehicle clearance), SARATHI 4.0 (driver licensing), FASTag toll telemetry, FOIS rail status, and E-Way Bill manifests.

4. Arrhenius Spoilage PINN

Physics-Informed Neural Network processing cargo temperature sensors and commodity HSN activation energy ($E_a$) to compute Remaining Shelf Life (RSL) and divert produce to APMC Mandis or solar cold hubs.

5. Delta-CRDT Offline Sync

Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types paired with embedded SpatiaLite database allowing driver devices in zero-cellular deep valleys to log data offline and auto-merge vector clocks on reconnect.

6. Bhashini Multilingual ASR

Quantized IndicWhisper / Bhashini speech-to-text pipeline enabling hands-free, high-contrast voice navigation in 10 regional dialects (Assamese, Khasi, Mizo, Bodo, Manipuri, etc.).

Arrhenius Kinetic Spoilage & Dynamic Rerouting Physics

Biological degradation rates $k(T)$ for agricultural commodities vary exponentially with ambient cargo temperature $T$ (in Kelvin), triggering automatic rerouting to the nearest solar cold hub if Remaining Shelf Life ($RSL$) drops below $ETA_{destination}$:

k(T) = k_A * exp( -E_a / (R * T) )
RSL(t) = RSL_0 - Integral_0^t [ k(T(tau)) / k_ref ] d(tau)
